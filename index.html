§ 01 · Overview

About

I am a doctoral candidate in the Department of Materials Science & Textile Engineering at Your University, working on the physically based simulation of fiber-scale yarn structure — from ply geometry, twist, and crimp to the appearance models used in cloth rendering.

§ 02 · Research

Research Focus

“A yarn should be derived from how it was spun and woven — not sculpted by hand to look approximately right.”

The work progresses from explicit fiber-level geometry, through learned Gaussian yarn assets, to fabrics assembled from that asset library — each stage keeping process parameters traceable back to their physical source.

I

Fiber-Scale Geometry Synthesis

Generating monofilament, plied, and core-spun yarn geometry directly from process presets — twist, migration, crimp, and interlacing — with provenance kept separate from synthetic priors.

II

Yarn-to-Fabric Asset Learning

Compressing multi-view captures of canonical yarn bodies into reusable, editable Gaussian assets that downstream fabric assembly can relax, collide, and render at scale.
